How the score works

Score = 100 × Total ÷ Expected Total, where Expected Total is what a typical youth lifter of the same sex, age, and bodyweight totals at USAPL local and state meets. A score of 100 means a typical performance; recent Youth Nationals best lifters have scored roughly 140–180.

Expected Total = A × Bodyweightk × AgeFactor, with coefficients fit on 2,156 USAPL youth full-power results from 2020–2026 and validated against six years of held-out Youth Nationals. Unlike DOTS, it is valid below 40 kg bodyweight and adjusts for single-year age differences, so an 8-year-old and a 9-year-old in Youth 1 are compared fairly.

Scores are comparable across ages and bodyweights within Youth. The proposal awards best lifter within each division (Youth 1, 2, 3, by sex).

Status: committee proposal. Prepared for the USA Powerlifting Youth Committee, 2026. Valid for ages 8–13 and bodyweights roughly 18–130 kg (the range observed in competition). Not for use with adult or teen lifters.
